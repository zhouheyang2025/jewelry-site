ABOUT / BODY OBJECTS

Jewelry as measurement. Body as drawing. Object as evidence.

Body Objects is a conceptual jewelry project that treats ornament as a system of bodily measurement. Each piece is developed through technical drawing, material construction, and observation of wearable structures.

STATEMENT

The project begins from the idea that jewelry does not only decorate the body. It can mark distance, pressure, posture, attachment, and social perception. A wearable object can behave like a measuring device, a frame, or a visible sign of an otherwise invisible relation.

The work uses technical drawing, metal structures, body diagrams, and material tests to translate small bodily situations into objects. A clavicle distance, an ear fold, a hand gesture, or a line of tension can become the starting point for a piece.

Rather than presenting the body as a stable model, the project treats it as a shifting field of measurements. Wearing becomes a way of locating the body inside a system of lines, forces, and social signals.

PRINCIPLES

These four principles can also become the conceptual structure of the whole website. You can keep them, rename them, or rewrite them later.

01

Body as Drawing

The body is read as a surface of lines, distances, folds, and coordinates. It is not only represented; it becomes a working diagram.

02

Jewelry as Measurement

Ornament is treated as a device that frames, records, or exaggerates bodily information rather than simply adding decoration.

03

Object as Evidence

Each piece keeps traces of decisions: measurements, tests, structural limits, and unresolved tensions between body and material.

04

Wearing as Positioning

Wearing is understood as an act of placement. The object positions the body within a field of visibility, distance, and social reading.
