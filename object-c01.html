CONCEPT NOTE

Clavicle Gauge treats the clavicle not as a decorative neckline, but as a measurable threshold. The piece translates a small anatomical distance into a visible technical line, turning the upper body into a site of reading, positioning, and calibration.

Instead of emphasizing ornament as surface decoration, the object works like a bodily instrument. It frames posture, breath, and distance as forms of information. The wearer does not simply display the object; the object also displays the body’s relation to structure.
