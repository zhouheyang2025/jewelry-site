ARCHIVE / STUDIES

Prototypes, body studies, and unfinished measuring systems.

The archive collects research fragments, failed structures, test pieces, and body-related studies that are not yet available as objects. These records remain visible as part of the working process.

ARCHIVE INDEX

This page can include incomplete works, material experiments, early prototypes, body diagrams, and conceptual directions that may later become finished objects.

A-01 Body study
BREATH / DISTANCE

Breath Measuring Study

A study of breathing as a measurable movement across the upper body. This project investigates how a small expansion of the chest could be translated into a wearable line.

Status
Research / In development
Possible Form
Necklace / body frame / clavicle object
Keywords
Breath, distance, tension, body signal
A-02 Material test
CABLE / MEMORY

Cable Memory Test

A material test exploring coated steel cable as a flexible but unstable structural line. The cable records pressure, tension, and failure through deformation.

Status
Material test
Possible Form
Adjustable necklace / moving connector
Keywords
Flexibility, tension, migration, instability
A-03 Diagram
EAR / ATTACHMENT

Ear Attachment Diagram

A diagrammatic study of the ear as a field of points, folds, edges, and attachment possibilities. The ornament is treated as a coordinate system rather than a decoration.

Status
Sketch / diagram
Possible Form
Ear cuff / earring / measuring frame
Keywords
Coordinate, fold, attachment, profile
A-04 Prototype
HAND / LIMIT

Hand Limit Prototype

A hand-based object that observes how fingers, joints, and gestures create temporary limits. The piece may develop into a ring system or a small wearable measuring frame.

Status
Prototype / unresolved
Possible Form
Ring / hand object / gesture device
Keywords
Gesture, joint, limit, measurement

NOTE

Archive items are not presented as finished products. They function as evidence of decisions, tests, interruptions, and possible directions. Some may later become available objects; others remain as records of a method.
