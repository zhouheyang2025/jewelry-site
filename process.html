PROCESS / METHOD

From body observation to technical drawing, material test, and wearing calibration.

The objects are developed through a sequence of observation, measurement, drawing, modeling, making, and testing. The process remains part of the final work.

METHOD MAP

This structure can be used to describe each project later. You can replace the text with specific notes, images, diagrams, and production records from your own work.

01

Observe

The process begins with a bodily situation: a distance, a pressure point, a gesture, a fold, or a line that is usually overlooked.

02

Measure

The body is not treated as a neutral model, but as a changing surface. Distances, tensions, and positions are recorded as relational data.

03

Draw

Technical drawing translates bodily information into lines, grids, angles, and structural decisions. Drawing becomes a way of thinking, not only a plan.

04

Model

Digital or physical models test scale, proportion, attachment, and the possible relationship between object and body.

05

Make

Metal, cable, soldering, welding, polishing, and assembly convert the diagram into a material object with weight and resistance.

06

Wear

The object is tested on the body. Movement, comfort, pressure, instability, and visibility are treated as part of the final calibration.
