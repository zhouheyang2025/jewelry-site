Method

Process

A short working sequence for object records.

  1. 01

    Observe

    Find a body line, distance, pressure point, or gesture.

  2. 02

    Measure

    Record position, scale, and relation.

  3. 03

    Draw

    Translate the body record into structure.

  4. 04

    Make

    Test material, weight, and attachment.

  5. 05

    Wear

    Check position, movement, and contact with the body.
